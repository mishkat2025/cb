## Summary

1. **No network predicts the physiotherapists' score within a group better than the within-group mean**, in any
   E01–E04 stratum, for the total score or the movement-quality score (MQ). Pooled over the E01–E04 experts, Proposed
   is +0.85 [−0.36, 2.23] RMSE against the group-only baseline (total score) and B1 +2.67 [0.78, 4.52]. The pilot's
   negative result (E21) holds for every exercise.
2. **Among the networks, Proposed (CVDC + λ 1.0 fine-tuning) is the most accurate for the experts**: −1.82 [−2.78,
   −0.82] total-score RMSE vs B1 pooled (lower in E02, E03 and E04 individually), and it ranks videos best within
   exercises (centred ρ 0.28 vs 0.11 for B1). For the non-experts it is worse than B1 (+2.55 [0.82, 4.42]).
3. **CVDC's clear, measurable effect is view consistency.** Within-group VCI: 0.51–0.58 right after CVDC and 0.41–0.53
   after fine-tuning for the experts, against ≤ 0.08 for a random encoder and 0.06–0.30 for B1. The distance of a rep to
   the PT reference (the "vector difference") becomes view-stable: ICC 0.55–0.78 for Proposed vs 0.13–0.45 for B1
   (Δ +0.28 to +0.56, all CIs above 0).
4. **Cross-view protocol (train on two cameras, test on the third):** on the camera it never saw, B1 loses accuracy
   (experts: total-score RMSE +4.68 [3.36, 6.08] over the seen cameras, MQ +1.72), while Proposed loses much less
   (+1.37 [0.39, 2.39]; MQ +0.26 [−0.03, 0.68]); the difference of the two gaps is −3.32 [−4.20, −2.37] (MQ −1.46).
   On the held-out camera Proposed is 5.4 points more accurate than B1 (total score), but **still worse than the
   group mean** (+2.64 [1.15, 4.12]). Its embedding stays view-consistent on the unseen camera (VCI 0.41 vs 0.15 for
   B1) and so does the distance to the PT reference (ICC 0.27–0.74 vs 0.00–0.16 for B1, all paired CIs above 0).
5. **The expert / non-expert groups are separable from every encoder's embeddings, including an untrained one**
   (AUC E01 0.95–0.98, E03 0.72–0.83, pooled 0.87–0.92). Pooled results would mainly measure the group (clip length,
   recording set-up), which is why every result is reported within groups.
6. **Module III (rule-based, against the PT demo)** agrees weakly and positively with the physiotherapists in most
   strata (best: E03 non-experts ρ 0.43 [0.20, 0.60], E02 MQ 0.34 [0.12, 0.52]), never strongly, and does not beat the
   group mean as a predictor. The E03 range rule was recalibrated to the experts (M18): it no longer flags the best
   videos (38% → 7% of their reps).
7. **Demo:** a live two-panel demo (patient with skeleton, PT reference from rest to rest with live per-joint
   differences) and a four-panel export video (adds the estimated 3D skeletons and the CVDC embedding with the distance
   to the PT reference); held-out subjects; `demo/TEST_SHEET.md`.
8. **LOSO (§7, added 2026-10-22) does not change these conclusions**: still no network beats the within-group mean
   (0 of 24 comparisons), Proposed is still the most accurate network for the experts (−1.88 [−2.94, −0.86] vs B1,
   −1.48 [−2.32, −0.77] vs B2-KTPFormer; lower error for 21 of 24 experts against each, Wilcoxon p < 0.001) and worse
   than B1 for the non-experts, and CVDC still makes the embedding and the distance to the PT reference view-consistent.
   No network's pooled RMSE changes between the two schemes (all paired CIs include 0).

**In one line:** CVDC makes the learned representation and the distance to the physiotherapist's reference
camera-independent, and it is the best of the networks for the experts, including on an unseen camera; but on this
dataset none of the networks rates a performance better than the group average does.

MobiPhysio: three phones (front, left, right), 3 physiotherapist judges × 7 EAAQ items per video (total 0–100; MQ =
100 × mean of PF1, PF2, CF1, CF2, CF3, D13). Experts (recorded 2024) did synchronised multi-view sets of ~10 reps;
non-experts (2022) did single-rep, mostly front-only, unsynchronised clips. Each exercise has one physiotherapist demo
video, used as the reference (Module III templates, the embedding reference and the demo).

## 2. Methods

- **Phase 1:** MediaPipe Pose (full model, 960 px) → 30 fps grid, interpolation, smoothing, root (mid-hip) and torso
  normalisation; rep segmentation per exercise (P6); views synchronised in software (audio, creation time, motion).
- **Models (one per exercise, T17):** B1 = ST-GCN on 2D keypoints from scratch; B2-KTPFormer = the same on
  KTPFormer-lifted 3D; **Proposed** = the B1 encoder pretrained with the cross-view contrastive loss (CVDC: the same rep
  seen by two cameras is a positive pair; synced, audio-lag, rep-matched and synthetic-view pairs) and then fine-tuned
  jointly (supervised + CVDC, λ 1.0); Proposed frozen = the CVDC encoder with only the heads trained. Rep head + video
  head (set score and items). No exercise head.
- **Module III:** DTW alignment of each rep to the PT demo's template, per-channel 3D-angle deviations against
  per-view targets and expert bands, rules (range, posture, speed), set score.

## 3. Evaluation

GroupKFold-5 per exercise, grouped by subject and balanced for expert / non-expert; CVDC pretraining inside each fold
on training subjects only. Video level; **within groups first** (each exercise × group), pooled over E01–E04 within
each group second; 95% CIs from the subject bootstrap (2,000 draws), paired for differences. Baselines: predict-the-
mean, group-only (within-group training mean), duration-only, rep-count-only, set features. Cross-view protocol: every
held-out camera × method × fold (180 runs), the models seeing only the two training cameras in every stage
(including CVDC). VCI = within-group similarity of the same rep across cameras minus similarity to other groups
(centred 256-d embeddings). Distance view stability = ICC(1) of the standardised distance to the PT reference over
synced expert performances.

## 5. Limitations

- **Four shoulder exercises, one body region**, two of them (E02, E04) with experts only, so the expert / non-expert
  contrast exists only in E01 and E03, and there it is confounded with clip length, views, recording year, location
  and phones (E9–E12).
- **Small samples**: 24 experts per exercise (the same 24 people), 25–26 non-experts at rep level in E01 / E03, with
  the non-experts mostly single-view and unsynchronised (few cross-view pairs for CVDC).
- **Skewed, narrow expert scores**, dominated by the repetition items (PF3 / PF4), with an inter-view label-noise floor
  of 2.5–5.3 points; within-group score prediction is hard for any method.
- **3 views, synchronisation reconstructed in software**; no error-type labels (Module III errors are rule-based and
  judged by their agreement with the scores); **3D angles from a single camera** are view-dependent (Module III's
  measures and the demo's 3D panel are estimates).
- **PT references**: one demo per exercise; E03's V3 demo exceeds most experts' end range (hence M18) and lowers the
  arm between reps; E04's demo is filmed obliquely and brings the arm down to the side at its peak.
- **Post-hoc choices** are labelled as such (M18 calibration, X7–X9 demo display); the GroupKFold-5 scheme is the final
  table (LOSO was not run, E1). *2026-10-22: LOSO was run afterwards for the networks (§7); the cross-view protocol,
  Module III and the group probe stay GroupKFold-5 only.*

## 7. LOSO per exercise (added 2026-10-22)

**What was run.** Leave-one-subject-out within each exercise, one model per exercise as in §2: every subject with ≥ 1
rep is the test set once (E01 50 folds: 24 experts / 26 non-experts; E02 24; E03 49: 24 / 25; E04 24 = 147 folds),
for B1, B2-KTPFormer, Proposed (λ 1.0) and the frozen Proposed (588 runs, 19.3 h on one GPU; estimated 19.0 h from a
smoke test). Every setting is the GroupKFold-5 runs' (early stopping on 20% of each group's training subjects,
video head, equal video weights, targets standardised per fold, no exercise head, E03 P02 in score regression); only
the split changes. **CVDC shortcut (C7):** LOSO runs no CVDC pretraining; both Proposed variants of subject s start
from the CVDC encoder of the GroupKFold-5 fold that held s out. Checked for all 147 subjects before training and in
all 294 Proposed runs: s was in that encoder's test fold and never among the subjects it trained or early-stopped
on, and every subject the encoder saw is a training subject of the LOSO fold (no leakage). So the frozen Proposed's
embedding is the GroupKFold-5 encoder's (only its heads are new); Proposed λ 1.0 is fine-tuned anew in every fold.
Same metrics, baselines (refitted on each LOSO fold's training videos) and subject bootstrap as §3, plus per-subject
paired tests (each subject's RMSE over its videos; Wilcoxon signed-rank, Holm over the six within-exercise strata;
rank-biserial r). The cross-view protocol, Module III and the group probe were not repeated. Full tables:
[`final_E01_E04_loso_tables.md`](final_E01_E04_loso_tables.md) (generated by `scripts/full/loso/report_loso.py`; decision
E30).

## 8. Proposal completion (added 2026-10-22)

**Scope.** The items of the proposal that were not, or only partly, implemented, leaving aside what the dataset rules
out (no new participants, exercises or fourth camera; no per-rep, error-type or metadata labels; no 3D ground truth,
so no MPJPE), the final scope E01–E04 and one model per exercise (supervisor). Every number below comes from
[`completion_tables.md`](completion_tables.md) (generated); decisions D19, A11, M19, M20, E31, E32.

### 8.1 Summary

1. **Quality class (precision / recall / F1 / accuracy).** On the D6 classes derived from the score, every network is
   weak: pooled over the E01–E04 experts under LOSO, Proposed's quality head reaches accuracy 0.60, macro F1 0.37
   [0.34, 0.39], κ 0.14 (B1 0.57 / 0.35 / 0.09; the binned group mean 0.54 / 0.30 / −0.03); for the non-experts every
   network's κ is ≤ 0, while the binned set-features baseline reaches 0.61 / 0.46 / 0.08.
2. **Recording conditions.** For the experts, no condition (medium or low light, low or high jitter, occlusion) makes
   any network significantly less accurate than full light (all ΔRMSE CIs include 0; Proposed under high jitter
   +2.23 [−0.18, 4.96]). Module III's agreement falls under occlusion (experts ρ −0.15 [−0.33, 0.03] vs 0.25
   [0.07, 0.39] in full light). Pose detection drops only in the left view under low light (0.88) and high jitter (0.84).
3. **Temporal stability.** KTPFormer's lifted 3D is the steadiest skeleton (rest jitter 0.003 torso lengths in the
   front view, vs 0.005 for the networks' smoothed 2D input, 0.007 raw 2D and 0.010 raw MediaPipe 3D); high jitter and
   occlusion raise the raw 2D jitter about 8× (0.006 → 0.044 / 0.046). Rep to rep within one set, Proposed's predicted
   score varies more than B1's (median SD 2.45 vs 1.40 points); Module III's rep score varies most (3.02).
4. **Is the CVDC embedding depth-aware?** For the same rep, the side camera measures how far the moving hand travels
   in depth for the front camera. From the front view alone, the CVDC embeddings predict that depth excursion better
   than B1 (pooled ΔR² +0.10 [0.03, 0.16] Proposed, +0.11 [0.06, 0.17] frozen CVDC encoder), as well as KTPFormer's
   explicit 3D (+0.11 [0.05, 0.17]), but the absolute R² stays small (≤ 0.04 pooled, ≤ 0.16 in E02). The embedding
   carries a little depth information; "depth-aware" holds only in this weak sense.
5. **Module III with the CVDC representation (M19) and angular velocity (M20).** The proposal's pipeline (Fig. 3)
   now runs end to end: reps are aligned to the PT template in the Proposed model's embedding space, the joint angles
   are compared along that alignment, and the embedding distance and the angular-velocity deviation are scored and
   localised. Agreement with the physiotherapists does not change: pooled experts ρ 0.15 → 0.14 (Δ −0.00 [−0.04,
   0.03]) under GroupKFold-5 and 0.15 → 0.17 (Δ +0.01 [−0.04, 0.06]) under LOSO; the CVDC alignment alone lowers E03
   experts' agreement (Δ −0.07 [−0.12, −0.02]). The new embedding rule flags 84% of the E01 non-experts' reps against
   15–20% of the experts', but it is not more frequent in the experts' weaker videos (< 80) than in their best (≥ 95).
6. **B2-DepthAnything** (baseline 2 with DepthAnything depth, A11): *running at the time of writing (PROGRESS §0);
   §8.4 and the tables' section 6 are filled in from `depth_baseline_report.py` when it finishes.*

**In one line:** the proposal's methodology is now implemented in full on E01–E04 (apart from the parts that need
people or new data), and the added parts confirm the earlier conclusion: CVDC makes the representation consistent
across cameras and carries a little depth information, but no part of the system judges a performance better than
the group average does on this dataset.

### 8.2 What was added, briefly

- **Measures (E31):** `scripts/full/completion/classification_conditions.py`, `temporal_stability.py`,
  `depth_check.py` (the last after `encode.py --z`, which encodes every rep with every fold model and checks each model
  against the embeddings it saved in training).
- **Module III (M19, M20):** `configs/completion.yaml`; `cvdc/assess/cvdc_join.py` (per-time CVDC features,
  `STGCN.sequence`, alignment in the embedding space, frame path); `cvdc.assess.run --join TAG --velocity on|off
  --loso`. With both switched off the changed code reproduces the published Module III exactly (13,598 reps, max
  difference 0).
- **DepthAnything (A11):** Depth-Anything-V2 Small (Apache-2.0 code and weights), `cvdc/models/depth.py`;
  `depth_extract.py`, `depth_pack.py`; method `b2_depthanything`; `depth_chain.py` runs extraction, packing and both
  training schemes as one hidden, resumable job.
- **LOSO (E32):** Module III (published and completed) with per-subject libraries; B2-DepthAnything. Not added, as
  the proposal does not require them: CVDC pretraining per LOSO subject (C7 shortcut kept) and the cross-view protocol
  under LOSO.

### 8.3 Limitations of the added parts

- The quality classes are cut from the score, not rated by the physiotherapists, so the classification metrics
  repeat the score results in another form.
- The depth check's reference is the side camera's 2D view of the same rep: a real measurement, but noisy (the side
  view's own keypoint error, an arm partly hidden behind the body) and only one depth direction (forward).
- Module III's new rules have no error labels to be checked against (as before); their rates are reported.
- Still not done, because they need people: a usability study and a physiotherapist review of the feedback. The
  live-camera test is replaced by videos the user records and uploads to the Streamlit app.
