# Design decisions

Every choice the proposal, the dataset paper or the data leave open, with one line of reasoning. Each is configurable: Phase 1 keys live in `configs/data.yaml` and `configs/exercises/EXX.yaml`; keys for later phases are the intended YAML names. "proposed (measured)" = my choice, backed by a Phase 1 measurement, still open to change.

**Status:**
- **decided:** approved by the supervisor, or fixed by the spec
- **proposed:** my recommendation, open to change
- **TBD:** to be set in the phase named

| P6b | **Synced groups get identical rep boundaries in every aligned view** (supervisor, 2026-10-17): reps are segmented on the aligned view with the best signal quality (share of frames with both shoulders, both hips and the moving elbow and wrist visible ≥ 0.5; ties F > R > L) and transferred to the other views with the group's offsets; the group's moving arm is the best view's. Not-synced, unknown and single-view videos keep per-view segmentation. | Cross-view agreement in synced groups: 100% by construction (before: exact 32% / 15%, ±1 69% / 42% for E01 / E02); transferred boundaries coincide on the aligned clock within 0.96 frames. Best view: F in 116/127 (E01) and 115/127 (E02) groups. Rep counts changed in 86 (E01) and 108 (E02) of 127 groups (median 1 and 2 reps over the group's views, p90 5.4 and 9.4); 96 groups per exercise lost an edge rep that some view did not record (360 and 356 rep instances summed over views for E01 and E02). Training reps 7,827 → 7,095. Against PF4 (E01 experts) the set count does better than per-view counts: Spearman 0.36 vs 0.24; PF4 = 1 → ≥ 10 reps in 98% vs 88%. | `reps.view_preference`, `reps.transfer_tol_s`, `reps.transfer_keep: common \| covered` | decided (measured) |

| P8 | The moving arm is detected per group (best view), not assumed. | S2 | Audio: GCC-PHAT on 8 kHz mono, 100–3800 Hz, ±3 s window. | S4 | Refine lags with motion inside ±0.3 s of the device-corrected audio lag (±5 s when there is no usable audio lag). | S5 | Per-device audio-to-video offsets, re-estimated on motion-confirmed simultaneous pairs (\|ΔC\| ≤ 3 s): F→L +93 ms (n = 102, SD 58), F→R +57 ms (n = 77, SD 48), L→R −44 ms (n = 35). Step 0 had +126 / +36 ms from 8 and 11 pairs. ## CVDC (contrastive pretraining)

| # | Decision | Reasoning | Config key | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Positive pairs at **rep level**, with per-pair weights: (a) `sync`, `w_sync = 1.0`: audio-proven groups, and views of creation-time groups joined by motion-confirmed lags: reps with the same `rep_uid` (identical boundaries, P6b); (b) `audio`, **`w_audio = 0.8`**: views of creation-time groups aligned only by device-corrected audio lags (also same `rep_uid`); (c) `rep`, `w_rep = 0.5`: not-synced or unknown groups, reps paired by index within the same (exercise, subject, variation) and aligned by DTW on 3D joint angles; (d) `synthetic`, `w_syn = 1.0`: rotated views of each rep. | Supervisor change 2 and answer 3 (2026-10-17). ## Models (Phase 2)

| # | Decision | Reasoning | Config key | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Network input: **`xy`** (2D keypoints, 2 channels) for B1 and Proposed; **`xyz`** (MediaPipe world landmarks) for B2; `xyv` (x, y, visibility) selectable. In `xyv` mode a synthetic view copies its source view's visibility. | Synthetic views have no real visibility, and visibility mostly encodes the camera side (self-occlusion: AL left-arm visibility 4–20%), which works against view invariance. | `model.input` | proposed |
| A2 | Augmentations: in-plane rotation ±10°, scale ±10%, shear ±0.1, Gaussian noise 0.01 torso lengths, joint dropout 5% (joint to the root, visibility 0), temporal crop 80–100% resampled back to T (also jitters speed). The shared crop keeps positives time-aligned (the point of C1): "augment each view independently, but never break the pairing". `share_temporal_crop: false` gives independent crops. | `cvdc.augment.*` | proposed |
| A3 | Task heads are linear (as Yan et al.'s classifier), so a frozen encoder + heads is the linear probe. Exercise head: 9 classes (all configured exercises), from the embedding only. | A5 | **Pair builder** (`cvdc/data/pairs.py`): a rep set = a connected component of rep-level edges. Reps with the same `rep_uid` in a view pair of source `sync`/`audio` get that source. | A6 | Loss normalisation: L_i = (1/\|P(i)\|) Σ_j w_ij · l_ij, averaged over the anchors with ≥ 1 positive; weights do not enter the denominator. | A7 | Positive weights involving synthetic views: synthetic vs its own source view = w_synthetic; vs another real view of the set, or two synthetic views of different sources = min(w_synthetic, weight of the two sources' real pair). | A9 | ST-GCN spatial partition uses **full graph distances** to the centre joint (nose, COCO layout), so every neighbourhood splits into self / centripetal / centrifugal.
